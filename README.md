## 🚀 About Me

I'm a **Data Scientist** passionate about building intelligent systems and transforming complex data into meaningful insights and practical solutions.

With a background in Data Science and professional industry experience, I work across the machine learning lifecycle, from data preprocessing and exploratory analysis to model development, evaluation, and deployment.

My interests lie at the intersection of **Machine Learning, Artificial Intelligence, Deep Learning, and Agentic AI**. I enjoy experimenting with modern AI architectures, developing practical ML applications, and building scalable infrastructure for AI workloads.
